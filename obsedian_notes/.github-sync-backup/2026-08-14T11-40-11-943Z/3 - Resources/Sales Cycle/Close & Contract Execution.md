# 10 · Close & Contract Execution

> [!abstract] Goal
> Get a valid signature and a clean, correctly booked order.

|---|---|
| **Phase** | Getting to signature |
| **Owner** | AE + Deal Desk + Finance |
| **Typical duration** | 1–7 days |

## Process — step by step

### 1. Generate the final order form
Correct entity name, billing contact, term dates, quantities, SKUs, discount and payment terms. Check it twice — errors here surface as billing disputes later.

- **Metrics to measure:** order-form error rate · rework count per contract · % of contracts requiring an amendment within 90 days

### 2. Route for internal approval
Deal desk, finance and legal sign-off per your approval matrix before it goes out.

- **Metrics to measure:** internal approval turnaround (hours) · % of deals needing exception approval · approval bottleneck by function

### 3. Send via e-signature
To the actual signatory, with the signing order set. Confirm they have authority and are not on leave.

- **Metrics to measure:** time from send to signature · % signed within 48 hours · % sent to the wrong signatory

### 4. Drive the signature actively
Agree a signature date, then check in daily. Deals die in the signature window more often than anyone admits.

- **Metrics to measure:** follow-ups required per signature · deals lost or slipped inside the signature window · median days in signature

### 5. Countersign and distribute
Fully executed copy to the customer, to your CRM, and to finance.

- **Metrics to measure:** % of fully executed contracts filed in the CRM · time to countersign

### 6. Book the deal correctly
Close the opportunity, record ACV/TCV, start date, renewal date, term and any ramp. This feeds forecasting, commissions and the renewal motion.

- **Metrics to measure:** CRM data completeness at close · renewal-date accuracy (audit sample) · ACV/TCV accuracy vs contract · % of closed-won with all required fields

### 7. Trigger provisioning and invoicing
Create the production account, apply entitlements, raise the first invoice.

- **Metrics to measure:** time to provision (hours from signature) · invoice accuracy rate · days from signature to first invoice · DSO start

### 8. Say thank you properly
A short, human note to the champion and the buying group. This is where advocacy starts.

- **Metrics to measure:** post-close NPS/CSAT · % of new customers agreeing to a reference or case study at close

## Stage scorecard

| Metric | What it tells you | How to calculate | Directional benchmark |
|---|---|---|---|
| Win rate | End-to-end sales effectiveness | Won ÷ (won + lost) | 20–30% from SQL |
| Cycle length (SQL → won) | Overall velocity | Median days | 30–90 mid-market |
| Forecast accuracy | Whether the pipeline can be trusted | Actual ÷ committed forecast | 90–110% |
| Contract error rate | Deal-desk hygiene | Contracts needing rework ÷ total | <5% |
| Time signature → provisioned | First impression of delivery | Median hours | <24 hours |

## What you need going in
- Agreed terms from [[Negotiation & Objection Handling]]
- Approvals from [[Security, Legal & Procurement]]
- E-signature platform
- Confirmed signatory details

## What this stage produces
- Executed contract
- Closed-won opportunity, correctly booked
- Provisioned account
- First invoice

> [!success] Exit criteria
> Contract is fully executed, the deal is booked with accurate dates and values, and the account is provisioned.

## Where deals die here
- Wrong legal entity or dates on the order form
- Sending to someone without signature authority
- Booking incorrect renewal dates, which breaks the renewal motion a year later
- Going silent the moment it's signed
