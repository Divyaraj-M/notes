# 12-Month Objective — Build, then ROI

> **Context note.** This is the *why* behind everything in `_belki_files/Data/` (the 25 artifacts + 25 tasks already added). It records the objective, the two-phase structure, the metrics that matter, and — importantly — **how I arrived at this framing**, so the reasoning isn't lost. Read this before touching the task files.

Projects in scope: [[Sparrowcrm]] · IITM BS Degree · [[1layerx.com]] · [[Curious Geeks]] · Share Extension / Obsidian Plugin.

---

## The one-line objective

**Phase 1 — become undeniably valuable. Phase 2 — convert that value into income, a career leap, and recurring revenue.**

Not "let's make money." The destination is fixed (₹1.4L revenue + a major career step-up); the *path* stays adaptive based on what gains traction.

---

## Two phases

### Phase 1 — BUILD (Aug 2026 → Jan 2027)
Objective: **increase market value.** Everything here is a *leading indicator*.

| Area | Target |
|---|---|
| 🚀 Products | Ship 2 products |
| 👥 Users | 100+ active users |
| 💰 Customers | 3 paying customers |
| 🌍 Reputation | 25 public artifacts |
| 💼 Career | Feature Adoption Index ≥ 80% · own & ship high-impact features · build a portfolio of measurable outcomes |
| 🎓 Foundation | CGPA > 8 |

At the end of these six months, someone should look at the profile and say: *"This person operates like a PM who owns products, not just writes PRDs."*

### Phase 2 — ROI (Jan 2027 → Jun 2027)
Objective: **cash in.** Two separate engines run in parallel.

| Engine | Target |
|---|---|
| 💰 Products | ₹15k MRR (minimum) · ₹1,40,000 total revenue · grow paying customers |
| 💼 Career | A ₹20 LPA-level PM role — i.e. *become someone companies pay ₹20 LPA for* |
| 🚀 Product growth | Turn the best performer into a sustainable business · ship V2 on feedback |
| 🌍 Reputation | Grow the audience · get known for AI product building |

**Why two engines:** ₹15k MRR comes from products; ₹20 LPA comes from career. They compound independently — together they change the financial position far more than either alone.

---

## The 7th goal (added deliberately)

Phase 2's revenue goal is a *distinct* goal, not a continuation of Phase 1. Keeping it separate keeps the phases clean:

> **By 30 June 2027 — 💰 ROI:** Generate ₹1,40,000 in revenue from products and services built during the previous six months.

Phase 1 builds assets. Phase 2 extracts value from them.

## Metrics framework

**Track three numbers over the year:**
- **Revenue:** ₹0 → ₹1,40,000
- **MRR:** ₹0 → ₹15,000+ (built as ~3 small resilient streams of ~₹5k, not one product forced to ₹15k)
- **Customers:** 3 → 20+
- **Products live:** 2 → 5

**Outcome vs leading (career).** Don't measure only the salary — market conditions are outside your control. So:
- *Outcome metric:* secure a ~₹20 LPA PM role.
- *Leading metrics (within control):* apply to 100–150 quality PM roles · 30–40 networking conversations with PMs/founders/recruiters · publish 25 artifacts · ship 2 products with real usage · be able to tell 5 strong product stories with metrics.

Even in a slow market, executing the leading metrics directly raises the odds of the outcome.

## How I arrived here (decision log)

1. **Reframed the goal.** "Make money" → a concrete ROI target: **₹1.4L in Jan–Jun 2027.** A number you can steer toward.
2. **Split into two phases** so Build and ROI don't compete for attention: Phase 1 makes you valuable; Phase 2 converts value to income.
3. **Phase 1 KPIs are leading indicators** — products, users, paying customers, artifacts, FAI, CGPA. They create leverage; they aren't the payoff.
4. **Added a distinct 7th goal** for the ROI phase so the ₹1.4L target lives on its own, not smuggled into build metrics.
5. **Refused to pre-lock the revenue path.** Let the first six months surface where demand actually is, then commit budget/effort there.
6. **Added career as a second, parallel engine** — ₹15k MRR (products) and ₹20 LPA (career) are independent and both matter.
7. **Reframed the career goal** from "get a ₹20 LPA job" (partly outside control) to **"become someone companies pay ₹20 LPA for"** — earned through Product proof, Public proof, and Business proof, so interviews become evidence conversations, not potential pitches.
8. **Chose MRR as the durability test.** ₹1.4L is the short-term number; MRR tells you whether you built something that keeps earning.
