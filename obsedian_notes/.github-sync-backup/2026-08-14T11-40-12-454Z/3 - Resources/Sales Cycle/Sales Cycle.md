The full B2B SaaS sales cycle in 13 stages, from deciding who to sell to through renewal and advocacy. Each stage note carries the process step by step, **the metrics to measure at every step**, a stage scorecard, exit criteria and the common failure modes.

## The four phases

| Phase | Stages | The question this phase answers |
|---|---|---|
| Before a deal exists | 01–03 | Are we talking to the right people at all? |
| Working the deal | 04–07 | Do they have a problem worth paying to solve? |
| Getting to signature | 08–10 | Can this organisation actually buy? |
| After the signature | 11–13 | Did they get what they paid for — and will they stay? |

## The funnel maths

Read top to bottom — each rate multiplies into the next. Fixing the worst rate beats adding volume at the top.

```
MQL ──► SQL ──► Discovery ──► Demo ──► Trial ──► Proposal ──► Won ──► Renewed
     15-30%   >85% accept   >70%    >60%     25-50%      40-60%   >90% GRR
```

## The seven metrics that explain everything else

| Metric | Why it's load-bearing | Which stage sets it |
|---|---|---|
| Win rate by segment | Tells you where to point everything | [[ICP Planning]] |
| Pipeline coverage ratio | Whether quota is mathematically reachable | [[ICP Planning]] · [[Prospecting]] |
| % of opps with a dated compelling event | The single best predictor of a deal not slipping | [[Discovery]] |
| Multi-threading rate (contacts per opp) | Deal durability when a champion leaves | [[Discovery]] |
| Days in security & legal | Where mid-market cycle time actually hides | [[Security, Legal & Procurement]] |
| Time-to-first-value | The strongest early retention predictor | [[Handoff, Onboarding & Implementation]] |
| Net revenue retention | Whether the business compounds | [[Adoption & Value Realisation]] · [[Renewal, Expansion & Advocacy]] |

## How to use these notes

1. **Auditing the funnel** — read each stage's *scorecard* table and mark which metrics you can currently compute. The ones you can't are the instrumentation backlog.
2. **Coaching a rep** — read the *process* section and the *where deals die here* list for the stage the deal is stuck in.
3. **Forecasting** — the *exit criteria* callout in each note is the only honest basis for calling a stage complete.

> [!warning] On the benchmarks
> Every benchmark in these notes is a directional mid-market B2B SaaS rule of thumb (roughly $10k–$60k ACV). Replace each one with your own trailing-four-quarter baseline as soon as you have it — your own history beats any industry average.
