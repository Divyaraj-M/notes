<p>I am Divyaraj Murugan, a product sensical person who creates AI agents for work, and loves to build product from scratch.</p>
<h2>Two things I actually believe</h2>
<p><strong>Hit the inputs. Stop staring at the scoreboard.</strong> If the process is right and I'm showing up every day, the results take care of themselves. I'd rather get the inputs repeatable than chase a number.</p>
<p><strong>You learn by building it, not by reading about it.</strong> Every skill I have came from shipping something rough, breaking it, and working out why. Not from a course.</p>
<p>So what's written here is what actually happened, including the parts that didn't work. If you care about <em>why</em> the number moved, we'll probably get along.</p>
