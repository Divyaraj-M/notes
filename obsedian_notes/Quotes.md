- Do not clog intellect's sluices with bits of knowledge of questionable uses 
- ### Core Idea

Your mind has limited capacity. Don’t fill it with low-value information.

---

### What it Means

- Be selective about what you consume.
    
- Not all information deserves attention.
    
- Mental bandwidth is finite.
    

---

### Why it Matters

- Working memory is limited (Cognitive Load Theory – John Sweller).
    
- Excess noise reduces clarity and depth.
    
- Scattered knowledge weakens decision quality.
    
- Shallow input leads to shallow thinking.
    

---

### What Counts as “Questionable Use”

- Random trivia with no application.
    
- Trendy frameworks you don’t deeply understand.
    
- Endless scrolling content.
    
- Information not aligned to your goals.
    

---

### What to Prioritize Instead

- First principles.
    
- Skills tied to long-term goals.
    
- Deep understanding over surface familiarity.
    
- High-leverage knowledge (used repeatedly in decisions).
    

---

### Mental Model

Garbage in → Garbage out.  
Signal > Noise.
