# SparrowGenie Help Articles — Writing Schedule (v2)

**Pace:** 5 articles per day
**Total:** 56 articles (52 content + 4 landing pages)
**Duration:** 11 working days + 1 day for review

## Scheduling logic

Articles are written in the order a user encounters them. Day 1 covers what a brand-new user needs. By the end, we're writing export and settings articles that only matter once someone has used the product. This means every article we write can already link to articles written on previous days.

Troubleshooting articles are written on the same day as the feature they troubleshoot — not saved for later.
