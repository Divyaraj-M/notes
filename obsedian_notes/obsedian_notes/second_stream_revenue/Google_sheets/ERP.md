# Goal  and the Objective 
- The goal of this project is to give leadership instant clarity and control through one-page views, while quietly creating transparency and alignment for every employee underneath.

## Objectives (very important — this is what you’ll actually build)

### **Objective 1: L1 – [[CEO One Pager]]**

Give the CEO a **single page** that answers:

- What is happening right now?
- What changed this month?
- Where are the risks?
- Where do I need to intervene?

**No explanations. No digging. No follow-ups.**

---

### **Objective 2: L2 – Module Owner One Pager**

For each module owner (HR Head, Sales Head, Ops Head):

- Clear ownership view
- Live status of their domain
- Bottlenecks and delays visible
- Performance trends over time

They should never say:

> “Let me get back after checking.”

---

### **Objective 3: L3 – Employee-Level Views**

For every employee:

- Clear view of what they own
- What is pending
- What is approved
- What is blocked

Not to monitor them.  
To **remove ambiguity and back-and-forth**.

---

## What problem you are _actually_ solving (core truth)

You are solving **decision latency**.

- Leadership decisions are slow because information is delayed.
- Managers react late because data is fragmented.
- Employees make mistakes because expectations are unclear.

Your system fixes this **without changing tools**, only by **connecting them and structuring flow**.
