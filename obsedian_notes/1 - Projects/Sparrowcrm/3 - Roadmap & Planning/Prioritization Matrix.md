# Prioritization Matrix

> _Eisenhower-style: High Impact / Low Effort first. Score every feature before committing._

## Scoring Guide

- **Impact:** 1 (Low) → 5 (High) — How much does this move the needle for users and business?
- **Effort:** 1 (Low/Easy) → 5 (High/Hard) — Dev time, design, dependencies, risk
- **Priority Score:** Impact ÷ Effort (higher = do first)
