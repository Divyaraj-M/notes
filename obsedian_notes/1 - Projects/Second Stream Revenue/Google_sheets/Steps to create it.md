## Step-by-step: what you should do **now**

### 🔹 Step 1: Lock the CEO questions (NOT metrics)


CEO questions are stable.  
Metrics can evolve.  
This step gives **direction without rigidity**.

> “Is the business healthy?” will never change.

---

### 🔹 Step 2: Define the CEO one-pager as _empty boxes_

Important: **no formulas, no data yet**.

Just boxes like:

- Business health
- Risks
- Interventions
- Cash safety
- Capacity health

This prevents over-design.

---

### 🔹 Step 3: For EACH box, ask one brutal question:

> “What is the **minimum signal** required to answer this?”

Not perfect.  
Not accurate.  
**Enough.**

Example:

- To know “hiring health” → hires vs exits is enough.
- You don’t need 12 HR metrics.

This is where most people overbuild.

---

### 🔹 Step 4: Go bottom-up ONLY to validate feasibility

Now you go to L3:

Ask:

- Can this data be captured naturally during daily work? 
- Can it be auto-generated?
- Will someone actually fill this without friction?

If not → **kill or simplify the metric**.  
Not the system.

---

### 🔹 Step 5: Design L3 views ONLY to serve L2

Important rule:

> No L3 field exists unless it feeds an L2 insight.

No exceptions.

---

### 🔹 Step 6: Design L2 views ONLY to protect L1

L2 exists to:

- Absorb noise
- Resolve issues
- Prevent escalation

If an issue reaches L1, L2 failed.

---

## The mental model you should use

Think of it like this:

- **L1 defines “what matters”**
- **L2 absorbs complexity**
- **L3 generates signals naturally**

You are not building _screens_.  
You are building a **pressure-release system**.

---

## Why top-down is the correct anchor (and you’re right)

You’re right because:

- Leadership clarity is the product you’re selling
- Sheets already exist — structure does not
- Without L1, everything else is noise
- You want a replicable, sellable system

But you must **stop top-down at questions**, not answers.

That’s the nuance.

---

## One sentence you should keep on your wall

**Define the questions top-down.  
Validate the data bottom-up.  
Build the flow middle-out.**

---

## What you should do next (concrete)

Do this in order:

1. Freeze the CEO questions (already done)
2. Draw the CEO one-pager with empty boxes
3. Pick ONE module (HR is best)
4. Build L3 → L2 → L1 flow for that module only
5. Ship a working slice
6. Repeat for other modules

Not all at once.
