### What all things a CEO wants to know ?
# L1: CEO ONE-PAGER — COMPLETE BREAKDOWN

## 1. Is the business healthy right now?

**What the CEO is really asking:**  
Are we moving forward, standing still, or quietly slipping?

**Signals shown (top strip):**

- Revenue trend (this month vs last month)
- Cash in hand
- Net headcount change
- Order backlog vs production capacity

**Modules feeding this:**

- Sales
- Finance
- HR
- Production

---

## 2. What changed since last month?

**CEO doesn’t want totals. They want movement.**

**Shown as deltas:**

- ↑ / ↓ Revenue
- ↑ / ↓ Collections
- ↑ / ↓ Open positions
- ↑ / ↓ Production output
- ↑ / ↓ Inventory stress

**Modules:**

- Sales
- Finance
- HR
- Production
- Inventory

---

## 3. Where are the silent risks?

These are problems not screaming yet — but will.

**Red/Amber flags only:**

- Positions open > X days
- Payments overdue > X days
- Inventory below reorder level
- Production orders delayed
- Attrition rising

No explanations.  
Just flags.

**Modules:**

- HR
- Finance
- Inventory
- Production

---

## 4. Where do I need to intervene?

CEO attention is limited.

**System answers:**

- Top 3 bottlenecks
    
- Teams under pressure
    
- Processes breaking SLAs
    

**Example:**

- “Hiring stuck in Production – 18 days delay”
    
- “₹12L payments overdue > 45 days”
    
- “Stock shortage blocking 3 work orders”
    

**Modules:**

- HR
    
- Finance
    
- Inventory
    
- Production
    

---

## 5. Are we building or leaking capacity?

This is company health.

**Metrics:**

- Hires vs exits
    
- Time to productivity
    
- Output per employee
    
- Attrition %
    

**Modules:**

- HR
    
- Production
    

---

## 6. Are we cash-safe?

CEO thinks in survival terms.

**Shown simply:**

- Cash collected this month
    
- Cash pending
    
- Cash outgoing
    
- Runway (months)
    

No accounting jargon.

**Modules:**

- Finance
    
- Sales
    

---

## 7. Is execution aligned or broken?

Misalignment kills companies.

**Side-by-side comparisons:**

- Sales orders vs production output
    
- Production plan vs inventory availability
    
- Hiring demand vs actual headcount
    

**Modules:**

- Sales
    
- Production
    
- Inventory
    
- HR
    

---

## 8. What needs action NOW vs later?

The system must protect CEO attention.

**Buckets:**

- Act this week
    
- Watch this month
    
- Informational only
    

**Modules:**

- All (aggregated)
    

---

## 9. Is the company getting easier to run?

This is qualitative but measurable.

**Signals:**

- Follow-ups reduced
    
- SLA breaches trending down
    
- Faster closure rates
    
- Less escalation volume
    

**Modules:**

- All
    

---

## 10. Can I trust this data?

If trust is gone, system is dead.

**Trust indicators (small footer):**

- Last sync time
    
- % auto-generated vs manual
    
- Data owner per module
