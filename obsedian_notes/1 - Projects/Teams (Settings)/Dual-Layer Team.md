Each user has two types of team memberships:

#### Primary Team (Mandatory)
This team determines **default visibility** for all projects and resources created by the user.

**Rules**
- A user must belong to **one and only one** primary team
- All projects created by the user are visible to:
    - members of the user’s primary team
    - parent teams (via hierarchy)
##### Purpose:

- Manager visibility
- Automatic collaboration
- Consistent reporting
- Avoid visibility explosion
#### Additional Teams
Additional teams are used for **workflow**, not visibility.

### Additional teams DO:

- Allow section assignment by team
- Allow members to **claim** questions in that section
- Group users for collaboration and workload distribution

### Additional teams DO NOT:

- Give project visibility
- Give resource visibility
- Override sensitive content rules
- Auto-share project data

Additional teams support only **task-based workflows**, not access control.
