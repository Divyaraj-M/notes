# Start Here — Life OS Phase 1

## The only rule for the next 14 days
Use the daily note every day. Do not add anything new. Do not install Dataview. Do not build dashboards. Do not start scoring. Just use it.

The goal of Phase 1 is not progress on goals. It is **proving you will open this note daily for two weeks**. That's it.

---

## Your floor day
A day counts as "not broken" if all four happen:

1. Workout OR 15-minute walk (logged in Hevy)
2. One IITM study session (45 minutes minimum)
3. Read 10 pages
4. Log calories + expenses in the daily note

More is bonus. Less is a miss. Recovery rule: after any miss, the next 3 days do only the floor — nothing extra — until the rhythm returns.

---

## Your day shape

**Morning block (7:00 – 11:00)** — the protected zone, both keystones live here
- 7:00 – 8:00 — Workout
- 8:00 – 8:45 — Breakfast + read 10 pages
- 8:45 – 10:45 — IITM study session
- 10:45 – 11:00 — Get ready

**Work block (11:00 – ~10:00 PM)** — work + dinner at office. No habits.

**Evening block (10:00 – 11:00 PM)** — log + wind down
- 10:00 – 10:10 — Open daily note, tick boxes, log calories + expenses
- 10:10 – 10:40 — Read (optional second reading block)
- 10:40 – 11:00 — Reflection, wind down
- 11:00 PM — Sleep

If the morning block hits, the day is already a floor day before you even start work. That's the design — protect the morning, the evening just records.

---

## Sources of truth
Obsidian holds reflections and summaries. The raw data lives where it already does.

| Metric | Tool | What goes in Obsidian |
|---|---|---|
| Workouts | Hevy | Just a checkbox |
| Reading | Kindle + Bookshelf plugin | Just a checkbox |
| Calories | HealthifyMe or memory | Daily total only |
| Expenses | Manual (Axio if you want a backup) | Daily total + 1-line note |
| Weight | Scale | Weigh-in 3x/week (Mon/Wed/Fri AM), log number |

---

## Daily flow

**Morning (2 min):** Open today's daily note. Glance at the four floor habits. Go.

**Evening (10 min):** Tick boxes. Fill calories, expenses, weight (if weigh-in day). Three lines of reflection. Done.

## Weekly flow

**Sunday (20 min):** Open the weekly review template. Honest, short, no scoring.

---

## What Phase 1 does NOT include
- No mood / energy / focus scoring
- No 5-domain weekly scoring out of 10
- No Dataview, no fancy dashboards
- No Google Tasks sync
- No new metrics
- No PM career tracking (it's a Phase 2 add — for now your PM growth is happening through work and your weekly review observations)
- No PRD writing, case studies, etc. as daily tasks

These all come back in Phase 2 if and only if you've hit 10+ floor days in the first 14.

---

## The 14-day checkpoint
After two weeks, sit down and answer:

1. How many floor days did I hit out of 14?
2. Which of the four floor habits broke most often?
3. What's the one friction I want to **remove** (not add) before Phase 2?
4. Am I willing to keep this exact system for another 30 days?

If floor days ≥ 10, you've earned the right to add one thing in Phase 2.
If floor days < 10, the system is still too heavy. We cut, not add.

---

## Phase 2 (preview only — don't read until day 14)
Possible additions after a successful Phase 1:
- Energy 1–5 scoring (one number, not three)
- A second weekly study session block at the weekend
- A monthly review template
- PM learning slot (15 min/day, weekends only first)
- A simple finance review note (monthly)

Nothing here is automatic. Each addition must justify itself against existing friction.
