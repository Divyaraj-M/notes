## Three filters for ranking

**Filter A — 10-year impact.** Future-me 10 years out: what would I most regret skipping?
**Filter B — Multiplier vs. terminal.** Multipliers (sleep, workout, reading) compound across all other goals. Terminal goals (weight, CGPA, app revenue) are end-states.
**Filter C — Reversibility.** What can I start later with no penalty? CGPA from this term cannot be redone. An app idea will still be valid in 18 months.
