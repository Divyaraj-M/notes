## Self-Check

Before you ship the experiment:

- [ ] I identified a source domain outside my own that solved a structurally similar problem
- [ ] I mapped relational structure, not just surface features
- [ ] I stated a concise rule-of-thumb (heuristic) from the analogy
- [ ] I turned this into a falsifiable hypothesis with a predicted outcome
- [ ] I designed an experiment that can prove or disprove this in under 2 weeks
- [ ] I know what I'll learn whether the hypothesis passes or fails
