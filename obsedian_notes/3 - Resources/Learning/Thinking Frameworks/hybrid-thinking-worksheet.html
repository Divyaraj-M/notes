Discovery Cycle

1

Frame via Natural Language

▾
Strip away jargon. What is this problem really about, structurally? Describe the relationship, not the feature.
2

Generate Analogies

▾
Find a source domain where this structural pattern has been solved. Map relationships, not surface features. The further the domain, the more novel the insight.
3

Extract Heuristic & Conjecture

▾
Create a rule-of-thumb from the analogy (heuristic), then formulate a falsifiable hypothesis (conjecture). If you can't produce a testable conjecture, the analogy is decorative.
4

Apply Logical Validation

▾
Design the experiment. What would prove or disprove this in under 2 weeks? Think deduction (predictions), induction (patterns), abduction (best explanations for surprises).
