# Your 4-Section Video Framework

## 1️⃣ Introduce the Problem

Goal:  
Create tension.

Not theory. Not definitions.

Show the confusion.

Example:  
“You joined as a PM thinking you’ll drive strategy. But in your first week, you don’t even understand why half the features exist.”

Keep it specific.

---

## 2️⃣ Real Example Where It Fails

Goal:  
Make it real.

Use your story.

- You wrote detailed PRD.
    
- Designed flows.
    
- Thought you nailed it.
    
- Designer asked one question.
    
- Whole logic collapsed.
    

Example > Advice.

Stories create credibility.

---

## 3️⃣ Analyze Why It Failed

This is where authority builds.

Break it down:

- You optimized output, not understanding.
    
- You assumed user intent.
    
- You confused documentation with clarity.
    
- You skipped context.
    

This section is thinking depth.

Not emotional.  
Not dramatic.  
Analytical.

---

## 4️⃣ Desired Outcome + Solution

Now give one clear shift.

Not 10 tips.

One.

Example:

“In your first 6 months, your goal is not to impress with output. It’s to build context before proposing solutions.”

Then give 1 actionable rule.

For example:  
“Before writing a PRD, summarize user intent in one sentence and validate it with design.”

That’s practical.
