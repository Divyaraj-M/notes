# 13 · Renewal, Expansion & Advocacy

> [!abstract] Goal
> Keep the revenue, grow it, and turn the account into a source of new pipeline.

|  |  |
|---|---|
| **Phase** | After the signature |
| **Owner** | CSM / Account Manager, with the AE for expansion |
| **Typical duration** | Starts 90–180 days before renewal |
| **Previous** | [[Adoption & Value Realisation]] |
| **Next** | Feeds back into [[ICP Planning]] and [[Prospecting]] |

## Process — step by step

### 1. Start early
Open the renewal conversation 90 days out for mid-market, 180 for enterprise. Check the auto-renewal and notice-period dates in the contract.

- **Metrics to measure:** % of renewals engaged 90/180 days out · median days-to-renewal at first contact · number of auto-renewal notice periods missed (must be zero)

### 2. Build the renewal case
Value delivered against the original success plan, adoption trend, support record, and what next year looks like.

- **Metrics to measure:** % of renewals with a value readout · renewal rate with vs without a readout · documented outcomes per account

### 3. Reconfirm the economic buyer
Budget holders change. Verify who owns this decision now and re-establish the relationship before you need it.

- **Metrics to measure:** % with a verified current budget holder · stakeholder turnover rate per account · % of renewals where the signer changed since last term

### 4. Assess risk and price change
Health score, competitive exposure, budget climate, and any uplift or true-up you intend to apply — justified with delivered value.

- **Metrics to measure:** forecast renewal rate by health band · average uplift achieved · % accepting uplift without churn · contraction rate (seats/tier reduced)

### 5. Identify expansion
More seats, higher tier, new departments, new use cases, add-on modules. Land-and-expand is cheaper pipeline than net-new.

- **Metrics to measure:** expansion ARR · % of accounts expanded · expansion pipeline coverage · upsell/cross-sell win rate · expansion CAC vs new-business CAC

### 6. Negotiate and paper the renewal
Same discipline as a new deal — terms, uplift, multi-year options, then signature well before the deadline.

- **Metrics to measure:** % renewed on or before the renewal date · average renewal cycle length · % multi-year · discount given at renewal

### 7. Handle churn honestly when it comes
Run a proper post-mortem, capture the real reason, offer a graceful offboarding, and stay in touch.

- **Metrics to measure:** gross retention rate · churn reason distribution · downgrade vs full-churn split · win-back rate · % of churn with a completed post-mortem

### 8. Activate advocacy
Case studies, reference calls, reviews, referrals, and tracking champions who move to new companies — the highest-converting outbound list you own.

- **Metrics to measure:** references and case studies produced · referral-sourced pipeline and ARR · review count per quarter · NPS promoter % · number of tracked champion job-moves converted to pipeline

## Stage scorecard

| Metric | What it tells you | How to calculate | Directional benchmark |
|---|---|---|---|
| Gross retention (GRR) | Whether the product holds without upsell | (Start − churn − contraction) ÷ start | >90% mid-market |
| Net revenue retention (NRR) | Whether the base is a growth engine | (Start + expansion − churn − contraction) ÷ start | >110% strong |
| Renewal rate by segment | Where retention actually comes from | Renewed ÷ up-for-renewal | Compare to ICP assumptions |
| Expansion ARR | The cheapest revenue you have | Sum of upsell + cross-sell ARR | 20–30% of new ARR |
| Referrals generated | Whether advocacy is systematic or accidental | Referral opps per 100 accounts | Track and grow |
| % renewed before due date | Renewal-motion discipline | On-time renewals ÷ total | >90% |

## What you need going in
- Contract dates, notice periods and terms from [[Close & Contract Execution]]
- Health score and usage data from [[Adoption & Value Realisation]]
- QBR history
- Expansion playbooks

## What this stage produces
- Renewed contract
- Expansion opportunities in pipeline
- References and case studies
- Churn post-mortems that feed back into [[ICP Planning]]

> [!success] Exit criteria
> Contract renewed on or before the renewal date, with expansion identified and the account contributing referenceability.

## Where deals die here
- Discovering the auto-renewal notice period after it passed
- Treating renewal as admin rather than a sale
- Asking for a case study only when you need one
- Not tracking where former champions went
