# 04 · Discovery

> [!abstract] Goal
> Understand the buyer's world well enough that the demo becomes obvious. This is the stage that most determines whether you win.

## Process — step by step

### 1. Prepare properly
Research the account, the person, recent news, their current tooling. Write 8–10 hypotheses you want to test, not a script.

- **Metrics to measure:** % of calls with pre-call research logged · hypotheses tested per call · prep time per call vs deal size

### 2. Set the agenda and get permission
State the plan, the time, and what a good outcome looks like for both sides. Ask to take notes and to ask hard questions.

- **Metrics to measure:** agenda-set rate · call show rate · call overrun rate

### 3. Map the current state
How do they do this today — tools, people, frequency, volume, workarounds. Get specific numbers.

- **Metrics to measure:** % of opps with a documented current-state workflow · rep talk-to-listen ratio (aim ~43:57) · questions asked per call · number of quantified data points captured

### 4. Quantify the pain
What does the current state cost in hours, money, risk, churn or missed revenue? Convert the complaint into a number **they** said out loud.

- **Metrics to measure:** % of opps with a quantified cost of inaction · average quantified pain value · ratio of quantified pain to proposed price (aim ≥3×)

### 5. Map the decision
Champion, economic buyer, technical evaluator, end users, blockers. Ask directly how similar purchases got approved last time.

- **Metrics to measure:** contacts engaged per opportunity (multi-threading — aim ≥3) · % with economic buyer engaged, not just named · win rate banded by contact count

### 6. Establish the compelling event
A date and a consequence. Contract renewal, audit, launch, board commitment, headcount plan. Without it, the deal slips indefinitely.

- **Metrics to measure:** % of opps with a dated compelling event · slip rate with vs without a compelling event · days between event date and forecast close date

### 7. Understand alternatives
Incumbent vendor, in-house build, competing tools, and doing nothing — which is the most common competitor.

- **Metrics to measure:** % of opps with a named alternative · win rate vs each named competitor · % lost to "no decision"

### 8. Confirm decision criteria
What must be true for them to say yes, in their words, in priority order.

- **Metrics to measure:** % of opps with documented ranked criteria · criteria-to-demo mapping coverage %

### 9. Recap and commit to a next step
Send a written recap same day: what you heard, what it costs them, agreed next step with a date. Ask them to correct anything wrong.

- **Metrics to measure:** same-day recap rate · recap-confirmation rate (did they reply?) · % of calls ending with a scheduled next step · discovery → demo conversion

## Stage scorecard

| Metric | What it tells you | How to calculate | Directional benchmark |
|---|---|---|---|
| Discovery → demo conversion | Whether discovery earned the next meeting | Demos held ÷ discoveries held | >70% |
| Multi-threading rate | Deal durability against a champion leaving | Avg engaged contacts per opp | ≥3 mid-market, ≥5 enterprise |
| % with quantified pain | Whether the business case can even be built later | Opps with a £/$ figure ÷ total | >60% |
| % with dated compelling event | Forecast reliability | Opps with event ÷ total | >70% |
| Talk-to-listen ratio | Whether the rep discovered or pitched | Rep talk time ÷ total talk time | ~43% rep talk |

## What you need going in
- Qualification notes and handoff summary
- Account research
- Discovery question bank

## What this stage produces
- Documented pain **with numbers**
- Stakeholder map
- Compelling event with a date
- Written recap email
- Agreed demo agenda

> [!success] Exit criteria
> You can state the buyer's problem, its cost, the decision process and the deadline — and they have confirmed your recap in writing.

## Where deals die here
- Demoing during discovery because they asked
- Single-threading on one friendly contact
- Accepting "we want to improve efficiency" as pain
- No written recap, so nothing is verifiable later
