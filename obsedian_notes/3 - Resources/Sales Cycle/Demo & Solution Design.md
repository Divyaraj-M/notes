# 05 · Demo & Solution Design

> [!abstract] Goal
> Show, using their language and ideally their data, that the specific pain you uncovered goes away.

|  |  |
|---|---|
| **Phase** | Working the deal |
| **Owner** | AE + Solutions Engineer |
| **Typical duration** | 1–3 sessions |
| **Previous** | [[Discovery]] |
| **Next** | [[Trial, POC or Pilot]] |

## Process — step by step

### 1. Build the demo around their criteria
Sequence the demo in the order they ranked their decision criteria. Cut every feature that doesn't map to one.

- **Metrics to measure:** criteria coverage % (criteria addressed ÷ criteria stated) · % of demo runtime spent on non-mapped features (target <15%)

### 2. Customise the environment
Their branding, their terminology, their sample data, their workflow. Generic demos get generic outcomes.

- **Metrics to measure:** % of demos customised · conversion rate customised vs generic · prep hours per demo

### 3. Open with the recap
Restate the pain and its cost, confirm nothing changed, then show. This frames everything that follows as a solution, not features.

- **Metrics to measure:** recap-confirmation rate ("yes, that's still right") · % of demos where scope changed at the recap

### 4. Demo in problem → capability → outcome loops
"You told me X takes six hours. Here's how it takes ten minutes. That's roughly N hours a month back." Pause after each loop and check.

- **Metrics to measure:** number of value loops per demo · buyer engagement checks per demo · buyer talk time % (aim >30%)

### 5. Bring the right audience
End users care about workflow, IT cares about integration and admin, the economic buyer cares about outcome and risk. Split sessions if needed.

- **Metrics to measure:** attendees per demo · distinct roles per demo · exec/economic-buyer attendance rate · win rate banded by attendee count

### 6. Handle technical questions honestly
Confirm integrations, APIs, data residency, SSO, permissions. Say "no" or "not yet" when true — it buys credibility for everything else.

- **Metrics to measure:** open technical questions per deal · median time to resolve · SE hours per deal · % of deals blocked on a technical gap

### 7. Trial-close
"If this worked exactly as shown, is there any reason this wouldn't be a fit?" Surface the objection while you're still in the room.

- **Metrics to measure:** trial-close attempt rate · objections surfaced per demo · % of demos with zero objections raised (a warning sign, not a good one)

### 8. Agree the next step before ending
Trial, pilot, security review, or proposal — with owners and dates. Never end a demo with "let me know".

- **Metrics to measure:** % of demos ending with a scheduled next step · demo → trial/proposal conversion · days from demo to next step

### 9. Send the follow-up pack
Recording, the recap, relevant case study, and answers to anything you owed them — within 24 hours.

- **Metrics to measure:** follow-up-within-24h rate · recording view rate · asset engagement rate · number of internal forwards (a champion signal)

## Stage scorecard

| Metric | What it tells you | How to calculate | Directional benchmark |
|---|---|---|---|
| Demo → next-stage conversion | Whether the demo actually landed | Advanced ÷ demos held | >60% |
| Attendees per demo | Buying-committee engagement | Avg distinct attendees | ≥3 mid-market |
| No-show rate | Pre-demo confirmation discipline | No-shows ÷ scheduled | <15% |
| Days demo → proposal | Momentum after the demo | Median days | <14 days mid-market |
| SE hours per closed-won | Whether pre-sales effort is efficient | SE hours ÷ deals won | Track trend, not absolute |

## What you need going in
- Discovery notes and ranked decision criteria
- Demo environment and sample data
- Case studies and the ROI model
- SE support booked

## What this stage produces
- Tailored demo delivered
- Technical questions logged and answered
- Documented next step with dates
- Follow-up pack sent

> [!success] Exit criteria
> The buyer confirms the solution addresses their criteria, and the next step (trial, POC, or proposal) is scheduled.

## Where deals die here
- Feature tours instead of problem-solving
- Demoing only to the champion
- Overselling a roadmap item as available
- Ending without a scheduled next step
