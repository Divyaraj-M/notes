# 12 · Adoption & Value Realisation

> [!abstract] Goal
> Make sure the outcome they bought actually happens — and is visible to the person who signed.

|  |  |
|---|---|
| **Phase** | After the signature |
| **Owner** | Customer Success Manager |
| **Typical duration** | Ongoing, whole term |
| **Previous** | [[Handoff, Onboarding & Implementation]] |
| **Next** | [[Renewal, Expansion & Advocacy]] |

## Process — step by step

### 1. Monitor health continuously
Usage depth and breadth, active users, key feature adoption, support tickets, sentiment, champion activity. Score it.

- **Metrics to measure:** health score distribution · % of accounts red/amber · DAU/MAU ratio · licence utilisation % · % of ARR with a current health score

### 2. Run the cadence
Regular check-ins for tactical blockers; quarterly business reviews for strategic value.

- **Metrics to measure:** % of accounts on-cadence · QBR completion rate · % of QBRs attended by the economic buyer · accounts per CSM

### 3. Prove value with their metrics
In every QBR, show performance against the success plan in the numbers from [[Discovery]] — not vanity usage stats.

- **Metrics to measure:** % of accounts with documented value realised · ROI achieved vs ROI promised · % of success-plan outcomes met

### 4. Expand adoption
New teams, new use cases, deeper features. Adoption breadth is the strongest predictor of renewal.

- **Metrics to measure:** feature adoption breadth · active teams / use cases per account · seat utilisation growth · % of accounts using ≥3 core features

### 5. Manage risk proactively
Champion leaving, usage decline, merger, budget cuts, unresolved escalations. Build a documented save plan the moment a signal appears.

- **Metrics to measure:** days before renewal that risk was flagged · save rate on flagged accounts · % of churn that was predicted in advance · open escalations per account

### 6. Close the loop on feedback
Route product requests properly, and tell the customer what happened to them. Silence reads as being ignored.

- **Metrics to measure:** requests logged per account · % of requests with a closed-loop response · median time to respond · shipped-request notification rate

### 7. Keep multi-threading
Relationships with one person are a single point of failure. Build coverage across the account, especially at exec level.

- **Metrics to measure:** engaged contacts per account · exec relationship coverage % · champion-departure detection time · % of accounts single-threaded (should trend to zero)

## Stage scorecard

| Metric | What it tells you | How to calculate | Directional benchmark |
|---|---|---|---|
| Net revenue retention (NRR) | Whether the base grows by itself | (Start + expansion − churn − contraction) ÷ start | >100%, strong >110% |
| Gross retention (GRR) | Pure churn, unmasked by upsell | (Start − churn − contraction) ÷ start | >90% mid-market |
| Product adoption (DAU/MAU) | Habit strength | Daily actives ÷ monthly actives | >20% for daily-use tools |
| Licence utilisation | Whether they'll cut seats at renewal | Active users ÷ licensed users | >80% |
| NPS / CSAT | Sentiment and referenceability | Survey score | NPS >30 |

## What you need going in
- Success plan from [[Handoff, Onboarding & Implementation]]
- Product usage data
- Support and NPS/CSAT data
- Health scoring model

## What this stage produces
- Health scores across the base
- QBR decks with proven outcomes
- Documented risk and save plans
- Expansion signals for [[Renewal, Expansion & Advocacy]]

> [!success] Exit criteria
> Value is demonstrated and acknowledged by the economic buyer **ahead of** the renewal window.

## Where deals die here
- First real conversation happening 30 days before renewal
- QBRs that report usage instead of outcomes
- Losing the champion and not noticing
- Treating adoption as the customer's problem
