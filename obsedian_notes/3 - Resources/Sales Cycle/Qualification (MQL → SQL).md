# 03 · Qualification (MQL → SQL)

> [!abstract] Goal
> Separate real buying situations from polite interest before an AE burns a full cycle on them.

|  |  |
|---|---|
| **Phase** | Before a deal exists |
| **Owner** | SDR qualifies, AE accepts or rejects |
| **Typical duration** | 1 call, 15–30 min |
| **Previous** | [[Prospecting]] |
| **Next** | [[Discovery]] |

## Process — step by step

### 1. Confirm fit against ICP
Company size, industry, geography, existing stack. If it fails here, disqualify fast and route to self-serve or nurture.

- **Metrics to measure:** ICP-fit pass rate · % disqualified on fit alone · % of fit-failures successfully routed to self-serve

### 2. Establish the pain and the trigger
What broke, what changed, what happens if they do nothing. No pain, no cycle.

- **Metrics to measure:** % of SQLs with documented pain · % with a documented trigger event · win rate of quantified-pain vs vague-pain leads

### 3. Test authority and process
Who else touches this decision, who signs, has the company bought anything similar before and how.

- **Metrics to measure:** contacts identified per lead · % with economic buyer named · % with prior-purchase process documented

### 4. Probe budget and timeline
Not "what's your budget" — instead: is there a funded initiative, a deadline, an event forcing action?

- **Metrics to measure:** % with a funded initiative confirmed · % with a compelling event and date · average stated timeline vs actual cycle length

### 5. Apply the framework consistently
BANT for velocity motions, MEDDPICC or SPICED for complex ones. Score the deal, don't just feel it.

- **Metrics to measure:** average qualification score · score-to-win-rate correlation · % of opps scored at all · score inflation drift by rep

### 6. Route the outcome
Qualified → book discovery with the AE. Not now → dated nurture. Never → disqualify with a reason code.

- **Metrics to measure:** MQL → SQL conversion rate · disqualification reason distribution · nurture re-engagement rate · % recycled leads that later convert

### 7. Hand over cleanly
Written summary in the CRM: pain, players, timeline, next step. The AE should never re-ask what the SDR already asked.

- **Metrics to measure:** SQL acceptance rate by AE · % of handoffs rejected (and why) · handoff-note completeness % · time from qualification to discovery booked

## Stage scorecard

| Metric | What it tells you | How to calculate | Directional benchmark |
|---|---|---|---|
| MQL → SQL conversion | Lead quality at the top | SQLs ÷ MQLs | 15–30% inbound |
| SQL acceptance rate | Whether SDRs and AEs agree on "qualified" | Accepted ÷ passed | >85% |
| % with compelling event | The single best predictor of a deal not slipping | Opps with dated event ÷ total | >70% |
| Disqualification reason mix | Feedback loop into marketing targeting | Count by reason code | No reason code = broken loop |
| Time in stage | Whether qualification is a call or a saga | Median days MQL → SQL | <3 days |

## What you need going in
- Lead record and full activity history
- Agreed qualification framework
- Written disqualification criteria

## What this stage produces
- Accepted opportunity (SQL) in the CRM
- Complete handover notes
- Reason-coded disqualifications

> [!success] Exit criteria
> The AE **formally accepts** the opportunity. Pain, at least one stakeholder, and a compelling event are documented.

## Where deals die here
- Passing anything with a pulse to hit meeting quota
- Qualifying on budget first and pain last
- No reason codes on disqualified leads, so marketing never learns
