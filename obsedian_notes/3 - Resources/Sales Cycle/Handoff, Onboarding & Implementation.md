# 11 · Handoff, Onboarding & Implementation

> [!abstract] Goal
> Transfer everything you learned to the team that has to deliver it — and get the customer to first value fast.

|  |  |
|---|---|
| **Phase** | After the signature |
| **Owner** | AE hands to Customer Success / Implementation |
| **Typical duration** | 2–8 weeks |
| **Previous** | [[Close & Contract Execution]] |
| **Next** | [[Adoption & Value Realisation]] |

## Process — step by step

### 1. Run an internal handoff
AE briefs CS/Implementation: why they bought, the pain and its cost, success criteria, stakeholders, risks, promises made, and anything sold on roadmap.

- **Metrics to measure:** % of closed-won with a completed handoff doc · time from close to handoff (target <48h) · CS-rated handoff quality score · number of roadmap promises logged

### 2. Hold a joint kickoff
AE attends so the customer sees continuity. Confirm goals, scope, timeline, roles and the definition of go-live.

- **Metrics to measure:** % with kickoff held within 7 days of signature · AE attendance rate · attendee count and role breadth at kickoff

### 3. Write the success plan
The outcomes they bought, the metrics to prove them, milestone dates, and who owns each. This becomes the renewal case twelve months from now.

- **Metrics to measure:** % of accounts with a documented success plan · measurable outcomes defined per account · % of plans signed off by the customer

### 4. Configure and integrate
Provision users, set roles and permissions, configure workflows, connect SSO and integrations, migrate data.

- **Metrics to measure:** days to complete configuration · integration completion rate · data migration success rate · number of implementation blockers raised

### 5. Train by role
Admins, power users and casual users need different sessions. Leave behind documentation they can self-serve from.

- **Metrics to measure:** % of licensed users trained · training completion rate · admin certification rate · support tickets per trained vs untrained user

### 6. Drive first value fast
Get one real, visible win in the first two weeks — the first survey shipped, the first report an exec actually reads.

- **Metrics to measure:** **time-to-first-value (days)** · % achieving first value within 14 days · day-14 active user count

### 7. Confirm go-live and hand to steady state
Sign the implementation off against the plan, then transition to the ongoing CSM cadence.

- **Metrics to measure:** time-to-go-live (days) · on-time go-live % · onboarding CSAT · day-30 activation rate · % of go-lives with open scope items

## Stage scorecard

| Metric | What it tells you | How to calculate | Directional benchmark |
|---|---|---|---|
| Time-to-first-value | The strongest early retention predictor | Days signature → first meaningful outcome | <14 days |
| Time-to-go-live | Implementation efficiency | Days signature → sign-off | 14–60 depending on scope |
| Onboarding CSAT | Delivery quality as the customer sees it | Post-onboarding survey score | >4.5 / 5 |
| Day-30 activation rate | Whether adoption started at all | Accounts hitting key action by day 30 | >70% |
| Handoff completion rate | Whether context survived the handoff | Complete handoffs ÷ closed-won | 100% target |

## What you need going in
- Full deal history and notes from [[Discovery]] and [[Trial, POC or Pilot]]
- Signed scope of work
- Implementation resources booked
- Training material

## What this stage produces
- Success plan tied to the outcomes they bought
- Configured production environment
- Trained users by role
- Go-live sign-off

> [!success] Exit criteria
> The customer is live, trained, and has achieved at least one of the outcomes they bought.

## Where deals die here
- Cold handoff with no context, so the customer repeats discovery
- Promises made in the deal that never reach the delivery team
- Onboarding that trains features instead of workflows
- Long implementations with no early win
