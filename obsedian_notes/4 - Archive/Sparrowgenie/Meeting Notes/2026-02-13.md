## What is Objective vs Opinionated

### Objective inputs (measurable)

- % of users exposed
    
- % of revenue tied to the flow
    
- # of dependent modules
    
- Error rate if it fails
    
- Support tickets from similar past failures
    
- Time to rollback (minutes vs days)
    

These are data-backed.

### Opinionated inputs

- Weighting of each factor
    
- What counts as “critical”
    
- Thresholds like 3.5 = high risk
    

Those are strategic choices.

## How to Make It Less Opinionated

Instead of weighted scoring, use **expected loss**.

### Step 1: Estimate probability of failure

Based on:

- New code vs refactor
    
- Past bug density
    
- Test coverage %
    

Example:

- Minor UI change → 5%
    
- Core workflow rewrite → 25%
    

### Step 2: Estimate impact if it fails

Use:

Impact =  
(# affected users × avg revenue per user × downtime hours)

Or

Impact =  
(Deals delayed × average deal size × probability of loss)

---

## Real Blast Radius Formula (Financial Framing)

Expected Risk = Probability of Failure × Financial Impact

This is not opinionated. It’s risk math.

Example:

- 20% chance of break
    
- ₹30L potential deal impact
    

Expected risk = ₹6L exposure
