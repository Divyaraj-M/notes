## 1. RFP execution breaks under deadline pressure

**What the buyer says:**  
“When an RFP comes in, everything becomes chaotic.”

**Problem:**

- Questions are long and complex.
- Teams scramble to get started.
- No structured way to break down work fast.

**Why this comes first:**  
This is the moment the buyer emotionally recognizes their pain.

---

## 2. No trusted knowledge backbone

**What the buyer says:**  
“We have answers, but we don’t trust them.”

**Problem:**

- Content is scattered across tools.
    
- No ownership or approval signal.
    
- SMEs get pulled repeatedly for the same questions.
    

**Why this comes second:**  
Before showing AI speed, you establish **trust**.

---

## 3. SMEs become bottlenecks instead of enablers

**What the buyer says:**  
“Our SMEs are always busy and hard to loop in.”

**Problem:**

- SMEs answer the same questions repeatedly.
    
- Context is lost in Slack and email.
    
- Their feedback is not captured for reuse.
    

**Why this matters:**  
SME time is expensive. Wasted SME time hurts scale.

---

## 4. Collaboration lacks clear ownership and flow

**What the buyer says:**  
“We don’t know who owns what, or what’s blocked.”

**Problem:**

- Authors, reviewers, and SMEs are not clearly aligned.
    
- Reviews happen late or get skipped.
    
- No clean handoff between roles.
    

**Why this matters:**  
Even great answers fail without coordination.

---

## 5. Work doesn’t compound across RFPs

**What the buyer says:**  
“Every RFP feels like starting over.”

**Problem:**

- Approved answers don’t strengthen the system.
    
- SME input disappears after submission.
    
- AI doesn’t improve with real usage.
    

**Why this comes last:**  
This is your **long-term value** close.

---
