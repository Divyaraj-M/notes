Teams can be organized into a **parent → child relationship**.

#### **Parent Team**
- A team can optionally have **one** parent team
- Parent team inherits **visibility** from child teams
- Parent team does NOT inherit **permissions**
#### **Child Team**
- Child team visibility flows **upward** to parent
- Child teams do NOT see parent team resources
- Child teams do NOT affect parent team assignments`

##### **Hierarchy Rules**
- No circular hierarchy allowed
- Each team has at most **one** parent
- Parent team membership does NOT auto-add users to child teams
- Visibility is derived, not membership
