# Turn your RFP into a winning response

Upload your RFP, generate answers from your Knowledge Hub, collaborate with your team, and export a submission-ready response.


![Overview of project workflow](screenshots/placeholder.png)


## Start with your RFP file

Create a new project and upload the file you received from the buyer.

SparrowGenie supports Excel, Word, and CSV files.

- Create a project
- Upload your RFP
- Let the system process the structure

If your file needs cleanup, see [Clean your file before importing](/help/clean-your-file-before-importing).

![Project upload screen](screenshots/placeholder.png)


## Map your questions

Mapping defines how your RFP is structured.

- Review detected sections and questions
- Fix anything incorrect
- Confirm mapping to proceed

You can learn more in [How to map sections and questions](/help/how-to-map-sections-and-questions).

![Mapping screen](screenshots/placeholder.png)


## Generate answers from your Knowledge Hub

Once mapping is done, generate answers directly inside the project editor.

SparrowGenie pulls from your Knowledge Hub to draft responses.

- Run auto-fill
- Answers appear against each question
- Each answer is grounded in your existing content

See [Auto-fill answers](/help/auto-fill-answers).

![AI answer generation screen](screenshots/placeholder.png)


## Refine answers and assign ownership

AI gives you a first draft. Your team completes it.

- Edit answers where needed
- Assign questions to owners
- Add comments for clarity

Use [Assign a question](/help/assign-a-question) to distribute work.

![Assignment and editing screen](screenshots/placeholder.png)


## Track progress and keep things moving

SparrowGenie shows how close you are to completion.

- Monitor the progress bar
- Identify pending or blocked items
- Send reminders to keep work on track

Learn more in [Track project progress](/help/track-project-progress).

![Progress tracking screen](screenshots/placeholder.png)


## Export and submit your response

Once progress reaches 100%, your response is ready.

- Export in the same format as the original file  
- Or export as a structured proposal (if enabled)

See [Export your response](/help/exporting-your-response).

![Export screen](screenshots/placeholder.png)


## What "done" looks like

You have:
- A fully mapped RFP
- Answers generated and refined
- Clear ownership across your team
- 100% completion
- A submission-ready file

From here, you send the response to the buyer and move the deal forward.

> **Good to know:** If answers feel weak, improve your Knowledge Hub content. That’s what the AI relies on.
