- When "Mark Image Locations in Spreadsheet" option is turned on during Excel export, every inline image in an Answer is replaced by a short placeholder such as an “image marker” that includes the image’s filename or similar text

- If you select **“Include attachments in zip file”** for an Excel export, all attachments (including inline images, PDFs, Word files, etc.) are exported as separate files inside the zip, not embedded objects in the .xlsx

### How Do I add attachments inside the project entry 

- To attach a file, you open the relevant Project Entry, choose the option to add an attachment, and then select “My Device.” You then either browse and add files from your computer or drag and drop them into the window, click Upload, and the file will appear in the entry’s Attachments area.
