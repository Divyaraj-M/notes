## SmartFill — what the articles say

### What SmartFill is

SmartFill is a feature that **inserts answers from a Loopio Project into webpages or portals**.

---

### What SmartFill does (explicitly mentioned)

From **Article 2 & 5**:

- Inserts answers into response fields in a portal
- Uses answers already entered and stored in Loopio
- Can insert:
    - All answers
    - Answers for a single Section or Subsection
    - A single answer

---

### How SmartFill is used

From **Article 2 & 5**:

Before using SmartFill:

- User must click **Refresh** to ensure answers are up to date

SmartFill actions:

- **SmartFill All Answers Into Portal**
- Insert answers for a specific Section or Subsection
- Paste a single answer automatically
- Copy answer text and paste manually

---

### Relationship with Refresh

From **Article 2 & 5**:

- Refresh ensures the extension shows the latest answers from Loopio
- SmartFill actions depend on Refresh being done first
