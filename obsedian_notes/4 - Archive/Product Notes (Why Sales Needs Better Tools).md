Objective : Need a tool that will help sales person to capture meetings better.

1. Why do they need such a tool ?
    a. In order to remember things better,
        Why do they need to remember things better ?
            I) So that they can focus on the meeting, not jotting things down.
                Why do they want to focus on meeting ?
                    -> To engage with the client and not do menial tasks at crucial moments with client.
                        Why do we want to avoid menial tasks ?
                            -> To be efficient.
            ii) So that no detail is missed due to human error.
                Why are the details important ?
                    For business efficiency, humans may miss out things that a transcript will not.
                        Why is efficiency on details needed ?
                            -> So that we can be accurate.
    b. To have an easy recap for next calls,
        Why do we want a recap for next calls ?
            i) So that we can follow up on the next call with precision.
                Why do we want to follow up with the recap ?
                    -> So that our clients/teammates can execute promised items on tasks.
                        Why do we want to execute on time ?
                            —> Maintain trust and deliver promise with client.
            ii) So that we can share we have not missed any points we had pushed for further discussion.
                Why do we want to not miss any postponed items ?
                    -> So that sales person will remember the blockers which stopped the customers to get our product.
                        Why sales person have to remember the blockers.
                            --> To make sure that we eleimnate the blockers on time for the easy process.
                    -> So that tasks are completed on planned timeline and not overflown.
                        Why the tasks should be completed on planned timeline and not overflown.
                            --> So that user can deliver the service that the client needed.
                    -> So that the assigned tasks of people can be tracked.
                        Why assigned tasks should be tracked?
                            -->So that user can ensure it is completed on time allowing to be proactive.
                    -> So that our goals are met on the decided timelines, to manage everyone’s time involved.
            iii) So that we can glance at what happened in the last meetings before joining the next one.
                Why do we want to glance at what happened in the last meeting.
                    -> So that we don’t have to waste time on manually remembering or checking our to-do lists on the fly.
                        Why do we want to remember things on the fly ?
                            —> For better readiness for the next call.
    c. To manage their history of calls to understand where what is.
        Why do we want to manage our call history ?
            i) For CRM sanity, and systematic approach to SAAS - admin end work
                Why do we want CRM Sanity ?
                    -> So that we can maintain a system of process and measure progress with company/team/individual objectives.
                        Why do we want a system in place ?
                            —> Business efficiency.
            ii) For easily seeing how our pipeline is like.
                Why do we want to see our pipeline well ?
                    -> To understand how efficient we are and where the gaps are.
                        Why do we want to understand our own efficiency ?
                            —> To improve our performance on calls.

    d. To profile the client and its journey,
        Why do you want to profile the client and their journey ?
            -> To quickly understand where they are , what they are
                Why do we want to quickly understand who the user is ?
                    --> So that we can be better prepared for the current meeting
                    --> So that we can know their journey
                    --> So that I know what actions/interactions we have planned on completing
                    --> So that we can structure our LOQs
                    --> To undertsand on what they really intrested in 
                    --> To know who all are the stakeholders
            -> To make sure their goals and needs are met
                Why do we want to make sure their goals are met ?
                    --> So that a good relationship is maintained, with trust
                        Why do we want a good relationship maintained ?
                            => To decrease the churn.
            -> To understand our sales pipeline
                Why do we want to understand our sales pipeline ?
                    --> To know and track our progress, in a quantifiable way. (templates)
                    -->To understand the timeline of the deal
