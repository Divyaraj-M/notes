# After Reset: New Credit Card System

Once the card outstanding is zero, start using the card only with this rule:

> Credit card spend must always be backed by the same amount of money already sitting in the bank.

## Card Payback Bucket

Create a separate bucket/account called:

> Credit Card Payback

This can be:

- A separate savings account
    
- A bank pocket
    
- A fixed Notes/Notion tracker
    
- A separate UPI account
    

## Rule

Every time you spend on the card, move the same amount into the Credit Card Payback bucket.

|Card Spend|Move to Payback Bucket|
|--:|--:|
|₹200|₹200|
|₹500|₹500|
|₹1,000|₹1,000|
|₹5,000|₹5,000|

If you cannot move the money immediately, do not spend on the card.

# Billing Cycle Rule

Your credit card bill is generated on the **17 th of every month**.

So your card cycle is:

|Period|Action|
|---|---|
|18 th to 17 th|Spend using card only if money is already reserved|
|17 th|Bill is generated|
|Due date|Pay full bill from Credit Card Payback bucket|

## One-Line Rule

> On the 17 th, the credit card bill should never surprise me. The money should already be ready.

## Never Do This Again

- Do not use the card for survival spending.
    
- Do not use the card if the same cash is not already available.

## Always Do This

- Stop card usage immediately if the payback bucket is empty.
    
- Review card balance every 3 days during the cycle.
