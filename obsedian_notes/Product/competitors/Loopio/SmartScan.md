#competitor_analysis 
## SmartScan — what the articles say

### What SmartScan is

SmartScan is a feature in the **Loopio Extension (new)** that **extracts content from webpages or portals** and uses it to **build a Loopio Project**.

---

### What SmartScan does (explicitly mentioned)

From **Article 2, 3, and 4**:

- Scans a webpage or web-based portal that contains a questionnaire
- Automatically identifies and extracts:
    - Sections
    - Subsections
    - Questions
- Adds this structure **directly into a Loopio Project**
- Shows a **summary of what was added** (Sections, Subsections, Questions)
- Allows users to **view the Project in Loopio** after scan completion

---

### How SmartScan is used

From **Article 2 & 3**:

Steps:

1. Navigate to the portal page
2. Open the Loopio Extension (new)
3. Select or create a Project
4. Click **SmartScan** / **SmartScan Portal**
5. Wait for SmartScan to complete

---

### If SmartScan fails

From **Article 2 & 4**:

- SmartScan may be unable to extract page content
- In that case, users can:
    - Manually select content to import
    - Report the issue using “Report Issue/Feedback”

---

### How SmartScan is positioned

From **[Article 1](https://support.loopio.com/hc/en-us/articles/360033770114-How-Do-I-Set-Up-the-Chrome-Extension)**:

- SmartScan is described as:
    - An **early sneak peek**
    - Part of the new extension

- Alternative algorithms exist under **Experimental Mode**
- These algorithms are:
    - Optional
    - Not required to use SmartScan
