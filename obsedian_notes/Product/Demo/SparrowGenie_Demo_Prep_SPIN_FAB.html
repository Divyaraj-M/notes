SPIN Selling Framework for SparrowGenie

Use these questions to guide the conversation from pain discovery to value realization. The goal: make Vipin feel the pain of the old way before you show the solution.

S

Situation Questions

Understand the current state

Goal: Establish facts about how the customer currently handles RFPs and proposals. Don't sell here — just listen and map their world.

  • Process mapping How does your team currently handle an incoming RFP from start to submission? Walk me through the typical workflow.
  • Tools in use What tools are you currently using for RFP responses? (Google Docs, Word, Slack, email, spreadsheets?)
  • Team structure How many people typically work on a single RFP? Who owns the final submission?
  • Volume & velocity How many RFPs does your team handle per month? What's the average turnaround time?
  • Knowledge reuse Where do your previously approved answers live? How do teams find past responses?
  • Decision making How do you decide which RFPs to pursue and which ones to pass on?
P

Problem Questions

Surface the pain

Goal: Get the customer to articulate their frustrations. These are the emotional triggers that create urgency.

  • Chaos under pressure When a time-sensitive RFP comes in, what breaks first in your process? Where does the scramble happen?
  • Knowledge rot How often do your SMEs end up answering the same questions across different RFPs?
  • Trust deficit When your team pulls answers from old proposals, how confident are they that those answers are still accurate?
  • Ownership gaps Have you ever had a situation where nobody knew who was responsible for a specific section, and it either got missed or delayed?
  • Wasted effort How often does your team invest significant time on an RFP only to realize mid-way that it wasn't a good fit?
  • Consistency issues Do different team members sometimes give slightly different answers to the same question across proposals?
I

Implication Questions

Amplify the cost of inaction

Goal: Make the customer quantify the business impact of not solving these problems. This is where the real urgency builds.

  • Revenue impact When an RFP gets submitted late or with inconsistent answers, what happens to your win rate? Have you lost deals because of this?
  • SME burnout If your SMEs spend 30-40% of their time answering repeat questions, what other high-value work are they not doing?
  • Scale ceiling If your RFP volume doubles next quarter, can your current process handle it without adding headcount?
  • Knowledge drain What happens when a key SME leaves the company? Does that institutional knowledge leave with them?
  • Compounding waste If every RFP feels like starting from scratch, how much cumulative time is your team losing each quarter?
  • Pipeline quality When you pursue low-fit RFPs because there's no structured go/no-go process, what's the cost of that misallocated effort?
N

Need-Payoff Questions

Let them see the solution

Goal: Get the customer to describe the value of solving these problems in their own words. Now transition into the demo.

  • Speed vision If your team could respond to RFPs 40-50% faster with AI-drafted answers from a trusted knowledge base, how would that change your capacity?
  • SME freedom What if your SMEs only got pulled in for genuinely new questions — and their past answers were automatically reused in future proposals?
  • Self-improving system Imagine every completed proposal automatically strengthens your knowledge base. How would that compound over 6 months?
  • Decision clarity If you had an AI-powered Go/No-Go scorecard before committing resources to an RFP, how many low-fit deals would you skip?
  • Collaboration clarity If every question had a clear author, reviewer, and status — and the system blocked you from losing track of ownership — how much rework would that eliminate?
  • Transition to demo Let me show you exactly how SparrowGenie does all of this. I'll walk through a real RFP workflow from upload to submission...

Before SparrowGenie → After SparrowGenie

Use this framing throughout the demo. For every feature you show, anchor it to "how they used to do it" vs "how it works now."

1. RFP Arrives — Getting Started

Before (The Old Way)

  • RFP document gets emailed around
  • Someone manually reads 100+ pages
  • Questions extracted into a spreadsheet
  • No structured way to assign sections
  • Unclear who is working on what

After (With SparrowGenie)

  • Upload RFP document directly into the platform
  • AI parses and extracts questions automatically
  • Questions organized into structured project view
  • Assign authors and reviewers per question/section
  • Go/No-Go engine evaluates deal fit before work starts

2. Answering Questions — The Core Work

Before (The Old Way)

  • Copy-paste from old proposals
  • Search through Word docs, Slack threads, emails
  • SMEs get pinged for the same questions repeatedly
  • Answers vary across proposals — no consistency
  • No confidence that answers are current

After (With SparrowGenie)

  • Genie AI drafts answers from the Knowledge Hub
  • Confidence scores show AI reliability per answer
  • SME-approved answers are reused automatically
  • Context info (prospect + competitor) available in-project
  • Instructions (prospect-defined + internal) visible to all

3. Collaboration & Review

Before (The Old Way)

  • Reviews happen over email or in Google Doc comments
  • No clear status: is it drafted? reviewed? approved?
  • Removing a team member orphans their work
  • Feedback gets lost in Slack threads
  • No structured handoff between roles

After (With SparrowGenie)

  • Assign → Draft → For Review → Approved lifecycle
  • Inline comments inside the question card
  • Role-based permissions (Author, Reviewer, Watcher)
  • System blocks removal until work is reassigned
  • Email notifications keep everyone in the loop

4. Knowledge Compounding

Before (The Old Way)

  • Proposal submitted and forgotten
  • Good answers locked inside the finished document
  • Next RFP starts from scratch
  • Institutional knowledge leaves when people leave
  • No learning loop

After (With SparrowGenie)

  • Mark best answers → "Add to Knowledge Hub"
  • Metadata tagged (category, product area, tags)
  • Approval workflow maintains quality
  • AI uses approved answers in future proposals
  • System gets smarter with every completed RFP

5. Decision Making — Go/No-Go

Before (The Old Way)

  • Decision made on gut feel or in Slack
  • No documented rationale
  • Teams pursue low-fit RFPs
  • Resources wasted on deals unlikely to close
  • No learning from past decisions

After (With SparrowGenie)

  • Three-layer scoring: EQS + ARS + PRS
  • AI Readiness Score shows knowledge gap
  • Past RFP Similarity Score benchmarks against wins
  • Structured questionnaire captures team judgment
  • Decision documented and auditable

Follow this sequence. Each step maps to a SPIN pain point. Use the talk tracks to narrate naturally.

Start by acknowledging the pain. Don't jump into features.

Upload a sample RFP document. Show how questions are extracted automatically. Then show the Go/No-Go decision engine.

Before anyone writes a single answer, SparrowGenie asks the most important question: should we even pursue this? The Go/No-Go engine scores the deal across three dimensions — how the team rates it, how ready our knowledge base is, and how similar this is to deals we've won before.

Before showing AI answers, establish trust. Show the Knowledge Hub as the backbone.

Most tools just have AI generate answers from thin air. SparrowGenie is different. Everything the AI generates comes from your Knowledge Hub — approved answers, SME-validated content, real deal history. The AI is only as good as your knowledge base, and we make sure that knowledge base is always growing.
4

Show Instructions Module

Open the Instructions tab. Show both the "From Prospect" and "For Participants" sections.

Before any SME starts writing, they see exactly two things: what the prospect requires, and what your internal team strategy is. No more digging through emails for compliance rules or win themes.
5

Show Context Info

Open the Context Info panel — show Company Info and Competitor Info.

Every answer your team writes should be tailored to who they're writing for. The prospect's company info and competitive landscape are right here, inside the project, so answers aren't generic — they're targeted.
6

Genie AI Answering — The Main Event

Show AI answering questions. Highlight confidence scores, the "Genie filled" label, and the ability to filter by high confidence.

Watch this — Genie reads the question, pulls from the Knowledge Hub, and drafts an answer. See the confidence score? That tells your team how much they can trust this answer. High confidence means the Knowledge Hub had a strong match. Your SMEs only need to step in for the low-confidence ones.
7

Collaboration — Assign, Review, Approve

Show the assignment workflow. Assign an author, show the review cycle, demonstrate inline comments.

Every question has an owner. The author writes, the reviewer validates, and the system tracks the status. If you try to remove someone from the project, SparrowGenie won't let you until their work is reassigned. Nothing gets orphaned, nothing gets lost.
8

Show the "Add to Knowledge Hub" flow from a completed answer. Show metadata tagging and approval.

Here's where it gets powerful. This answer your SME just approved? One click, and it goes back into the Knowledge Hub — tagged, categorized, and available for every future RFP. The next time a similar question comes up, Genie already knows the answer. Every proposal makes the system smarter. You never start from scratch again.
9

Close — The Long-Term Vision

End with the vision. SparrowGenie isn't just a proposal tool — it's the AI layer for the entire sales lifecycle.

We start with RFPs and proposals because that's where the pain is most visible. But as your Knowledge Hub grows and your team's workflow data compounds, SparrowGenie becomes the AI partner that helps your entire sales team close more deals, faster. Proposals are the beginning — not the ceiling.

Anticipated Objections & Responses

Be ready for these. Frame every response around value, not features.

"We already have a content library / use Loopio / RFPIO"

Those tools require heavy manual maintenance — someone has to constantly update the library. SparrowGenie's Knowledge Hub grows automatically from your real proposal work. Every completed RFP feeds back into the system.

The difference is: their library stagnates. Ours compounds. After 6 months, your Knowledge Hub has learned from every deal your team has worked on.

"Can we trust the AI answers?"

The AI only draws from your approved Knowledge Hub — not the open internet. Every answer comes with a confidence score. Low confidence means "get a human." High confidence means it matched a vetted, approved answer.

We built trust into the architecture. AI doesn't make things up — it retrieves what your team has already validated.

"Our team won't adopt another tool"

SparrowGenie doesn't add work — it replaces the scattered workflow of Google Docs + Slack + spreadsheets. The first RFP they complete will be faster than their old process.

Adoption is product-led. Teams experience value on day one because the first proposal is already faster.

"What about our existing proposal content?"

Your existing content can be uploaded to the Knowledge Hub. As your team completes proposals in SparrowGenie, the best answers automatically flow back in. You start with what you have and it gets richer over time.

Migration isn't a blocker. You bring what you have, and the system builds from there with every deal.

"How is this different from just using ChatGPT for proposals?"

ChatGPT generates from the internet. SparrowGenie generates from your company's approved knowledge, with structured collaboration, review workflows, and ownership tracking. It's not just about generating text — it's about generating the right text, reviewed by the right people, reusable for the next deal.

ChatGPT is a writer. SparrowGenie is a proposal operations platform with AI built in.

"Is this really ready for production use?"

Walk through the quality controls: role-based permissions, approval workflows, confidence scoring, reassignment protection. These aren't demo features — they're production safeguards.

Every feature is built for real-world use. We don't just draft — we enforce quality through structured workflows.
