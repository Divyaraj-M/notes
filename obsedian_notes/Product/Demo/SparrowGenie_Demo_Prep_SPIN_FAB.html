S

Situation Questions

Understand their current world

Goal: Map how the customer currently handles RFPs. Don't sell here — just listen and understand their workflow end to end.

  • Knowledge management Where does your team's institutional knowledge live today? Is it in docs, spreadsheets, someone's head, or scattered across tools?
  • Content quality When your AI or team generates an answer, how do you know it's accurate? Is there a process to validate and improve your knowledge base?
  • RFP intake process When a new RFP lands, how long does it take to break it down into answerable questions? Who does that work?
  • Collaboration model How do your SMEs, proposal owners, and reviewers coordinate today? What tools do they use to hand off work?
  • Tracking commitments During a proposal cycle, your team makes commitments — share a document, confirm feasibility, get legal sign-off. How are those tracked today?
  • Reuse & compounding After an RFP is submitted, what happens to the good answers? Can you reuse them in the next deal?
P

Problem Questions

Surface the pain

Goal: Get the customer to articulate frustrations. These emotional triggers create urgency to act.

  • Knowledge trust deficit When your team reuses old answers, how confident are they that those answers are still accurate? Has outdated content ever made it into a submission?
  • AI without a foundation If you've tried using AI for proposals, did it hallucinate or give generic answers because there was no curated knowledge base behind it?
  • Mapping chaos When a 200-question RFP comes in, how long does it take just to organize and map those questions before anyone starts writing?
  • SME bottleneck How often do your SMEs answer the same questions across different RFPs? Do they feel like they're repeating themselves?
  • Invisible commitments Have you ever had an obligation — like sharing a security doc or confirming a feature — fall through the cracks because it was tracked in Slack or someone's memory?
  • No learning loop After submitting an RFP, does your team systematically capture what worked? Or does every new RFP feel like starting from zero?
I

Implication Questions

Amplify the cost of inaction

Goal: Make the customer quantify the business impact. This is where real urgency builds.

  • Knowledge quality ceiling If your AI answers are only as good as the content you feed it, and that content is stale or scattered — what does that mean for every proposal you generate?
  • Speed to response If it takes 2-3 days just to map and assign an RFP before anyone starts writing, how many deadlines are you burning through?
  • SME cost If your SMEs spend 30-40% of their time re-answering the same questions, what strategic work are they not doing?
  • Deal risk from missed obligations When a commitment falls through the cracks — like a missed legal document or unconfirmed feature — what happens to the deal?
  • Scale problem If your RFP volume doubles, can your current process handle it without doubling your headcount?
  • Compounding waste If every completed RFP doesn't make the next one better, you're paying the same cost repeatedly. Over a year, what does that add up to?
N

Need-Payoff Questions

Let them see the solution

Goal: Get the customer to describe the value in their own words. Then transition into the demo.

  • Trusted AI foundation What if your AI could only generate answers from a curated, tested, continuously-improving knowledge base — not the open internet? How would that change your trust in AI-generated proposals?
  • Instant RFP setup If uploading an RFP automatically mapped and structured every question in seconds, and AI instantly generated answers from your knowledge base — how much faster could your team start the real work?
  • SME liberation What if your SMEs only got pulled in for genuinely new questions — and every answer they wrote was automatically available for future RFPs?
  • Commitment visibility If every commitment your team makes during a proposal cycle was tracked inside the project with clear owners and deadlines — how many dropped balls would that prevent?
  • Self-improving system Imagine every completed RFP automatically strengthens your knowledge base — the best answers flow back in, the gaps get flagged, and the AI gets smarter. How would that compound over 6 months?
  • Transition to demo Let me show you exactly how SparrowGenie does all of this. I'll start with the Knowledge Hub — the foundation that makes everything else work...

Before SparrowGenie → After SparrowGenie

Use this framing throughout the demo. For every feature you show, anchor it to "how they used to do it" vs "how SparrowGenie fixes it."

1. Building the Knowledge Base — The Foundation

Before (The Old Way)

  • Answers scattered across Google Docs, emails, old proposals
  • No way to test if your content actually answers questions well
  • Stale content gets reused without anyone knowing it's outdated
  • No process to find and fix knowledge gaps
  • AI tools hallucinate because there's no curated source of truth

After (Knowledge Hub)

  • Train: Upload websites, files (docs, PDFs), Q&A pairs, integrations
  • Test: Ask questions and see how well the KB answers them (external search or domain)
  • Improve: Knowledge Gaps module surfaces low-confidence and unanswered items for fixing
  • AI only generates from your curated, approved sources
  • The KB is a living system — it gets tested, gaps get filled, quality goes up

2. RFP Arrives — Getting Started

Before (The Old Way)

  • RFP document emailed around the team
  • Someone manually reads 100-200+ questions
  • Questions extracted into a spreadsheet by hand
  • Mapping takes days before anyone starts answering
  • No structured way to assign sections to people

After (Smart Mapping)

  • Upload RFP document directly into SparrowGenie
  • AI parses and maps all questions in seconds
  • Field mapping auto-suggests categories and structure
  • Genie AI immediately generates answers from Knowledge Hub
  • Team can start collaborating within minutes of upload

3. Answering Questions — The Core Work

Before (The Old Way)

  • Copy-paste answers from old proposals hoping they're still relevant
  • SMEs get pinged repeatedly for the same questions across deals
  • No context about the prospect or competitors while writing
  • Compliance instructions buried in email threads
  • Answers are generic — not tailored to who you're writing for

After (Genie AI + Context)

  • Genie AI drafts answers from the Knowledge Hub with confidence scores
  • Instructions: Two tabs — "From Prospect" (RFP rules) + "For Participants" (internal strategy) with AI summarization
  • Context Info: Prospect company info available inside the project
  • Competitor Info: Competitive intelligence per project for targeted answers
  • SMEs focus on low-confidence items only — the hard 30%

4. Collaboration, Ownership & Commitments

Before (The Old Way)

  • Reviews happen in email or Google Doc comments
  • No clear status: is it drafted, reviewed, or approved?
  • Removing a team member orphans their assigned work
  • Commitments (legal docs, feature confirmations) tracked in Slack or memory
  • Deadlines get missed, obligations fall through the cracks

After (Collaboration + Obligations)

  • Assign/Review flow: Draft → For Review → Approved lifecycle per question
  • Role-based permissions: Author, Reviewer, Watcher with clear access rules
  • Reassignment protection — system blocks removal until work is reassigned
  • Obligations module: Track commitments (legal, product, security, feature) with owners, deadlines, and status
  • Project Health: See overall completion status and what's blocked
  • Email notifications keep everyone in the loop automatically

5. Workspace — Individual Productivity

Before (The Old Way)

  • SMEs juggle multiple RFPs and lose track of their assigned questions
  • No single view to see "what do I need to do today?"
  • Switching between projects wastes time finding your tasks

After (Workspace)

  • Personal workspace shows all questions assigned to you across projects
  • Answer directly from Workspace without navigating to each project
  • Focus on your work — not on finding your work

6. What's Coming Next — The Vision (Roadmap)

The Remaining Gaps

  • No structured way to decide if an RFP is worth pursuing
  • Good answers from completed RFPs stay locked in the project
  • Knowledge base doesn't automatically learn from real deal work
  • No way to generate polished proposals from RFP responses

Coming on Roadmap

  • Go/No-Go: 3-layer scoring (EQS + AI Readiness + Past RFP Similarity) before committing resources
  • RFP to Proposals: Best answers flow back into Knowledge Hub automatically — system self-improves
  • Proposals: Generate polished, templated proposals from RFP responses
  • The vision: every completed deal makes the entire system smarter

Open — Set the Scene with Pain

Start by acknowledging the problem. Don't jump into features.

Every proposal tool promises AI-generated answers. But here's the problem: AI is only as good as the knowledge behind it. If your knowledge is scattered across old docs and Slack threads, the AI just hallucinates. SparrowGenie was built to solve this from the ground up.
2

Knowledge Hub — Train

Show the Train module. Upload a website, a PDF, Q&A pairs. Show integrations. This establishes the foundation.

This is where everything starts. The Knowledge Hub is the brain behind SparrowGenie. You train it by uploading your company's real content — websites, product docs, PDFs, existing Q&A pairs, even integrations with your existing tools. This is what the AI learns from. Not the internet. Your content.
3

Knowledge Hub — Test

Show the Test module. Ask a real question and show how the system answers it from the domain sources.

Before you use the Knowledge Hub on a live RFP, you can test it. Ask it a real question your team gets asked all the time. See how it responds, where it pulls from, and how confident the answer is. You validate the knowledge base before it ever touches a customer-facing response.
4

Knowledge Hub — Improve

Show the Knowledge Gaps view. Show low-confidence items and unanswered questions surfaced for fixing.

This is where most tools stop. But SparrowGenie shows you exactly where your knowledge is weak. Low-confidence answers, unanswered questions — all surfaced in one place. Your team can fix gaps proactively instead of discovering them mid-RFP under deadline pressure.
5

Project — Smart Mapping (Upload RFP)

Now transition to Projects. Upload a sample RFP document. Show how AI maps all questions in seconds.

Now that the Knowledge Hub is set, here's where the magic happens. Upload an RFP — could be 50 questions, could be 2000 — and SparrowGenie maps and structures every question in seconds. What used to take days of spreadsheet work now happens before you finish your coffee.
6

Genie AI Answering — The Main Event

Show AI answering questions. Highlight confidence scores. Show how it pulls from the Knowledge Hub you just built.

Watch this — Genie reads each question, pulls from the Knowledge Hub, and drafts an answer. See the confidence score? That's how much you can trust this answer. High confidence means it matched approved content from your knowledge base. Your SMEs only need to step in for the low-confidence ones — the genuinely new questions.
7

Instructions + Context Info + Competitor Info

Show all three context layers that help SMEs write better answers.

Before any SME writes, they see three things. One: the prospect's requirements — formatting rules, compliance conditions, deadlines. Two: your internal strategy — win themes, positioning, deal notes. Three: the prospect's company info and competitive landscape. Answers aren't just fast — they're targeted.
8

Collaboration — Assign, Review, Approve

Show the assignment workflow. Demonstrate the reassignment protection. Show Project Health.

Every question has a clear owner. The author writes, the reviewer validates, and the system tracks the status end to end. Try to remove someone from the project? SparrowGenie blocks you until their work is reassigned. Nothing gets orphaned. Project Health gives you the full picture of what's done, what's in review, and what's blocked.
9

Obligations — Track Commitments

Show the Obligations tab. Create a sample obligation with owner, deadline, type, linked question.

During every proposal cycle, your team makes commitments — share a security doc, confirm a feature, get legal sign-off. Today those live in Slack or someone's head. In SparrowGenie, every obligation is tracked right inside the project — with an owner, a deadline, a status, and linked to the specific questions they relate to. Nothing falls through the cracks.
10

Workspace — Individual Productivity

Switch to the Workspace view. Show how an SME sees all their assigned questions across projects in one place.

Your SMEs are often working across multiple RFPs at once. Workspace is their personal command center — every question assigned to them, across every project, in one view. They can answer directly from here without navigating to each project. Focus on the work, not on finding the work.

Anticipated Objections & Responses

Be ready for these. Frame every response around value, not features.

"We already use Loopio / RFPIO / Responsive.io"

Those tools are content libraries — they require heavy manual maintenance. Someone has to constantly update and curate the library or it stagnates. SparrowGenie starts with a Knowledge Hub that you actively train, test, and improve. Plus, we add the full execution layer on top: Smart Mapping, AI answers, collaboration, obligations, and project health.

Their library stagnates. Our Knowledge Hub has a Train-Test-Improve cycle that keeps it alive. And we don't stop at content — we run the entire proposal workflow.

"Can we trust the AI answers?"

This is exactly why we start with the Knowledge Hub. The AI only retrieves from YOUR curated, tested content — not the open internet. Every answer has a confidence score. The Test module lets you validate before going live. And the Improve module catches the gaps. Trust is built into the architecture.

We built a Train-Test-Improve cycle specifically so you never have to blindly trust AI. You verify it yourself before any customer sees it.

"Our team won't adopt another tool"

SparrowGenie doesn't add work — it replaces the scattered workflow of Google Docs + Slack + spreadsheets + email. Upload an RFP, get AI answers in seconds, collaborate in one place, track obligations inside the project. Workspace gives every SME a personal view of their tasks. The first RFP will be faster than their old process.

Adoption is product-led. Teams feel the speed on their very first RFP. Workspace makes it personal. Obligations make it accountable. It's not another tool — it replaces the broken stack.

"What about our existing content?"

The Knowledge Hub's Train module is designed for exactly this. Upload your existing docs, PDFs, Q&A pairs, even point it at your website. Then use the Test module to see how well it works. Use Improve to fix the gaps. You start with what you have and it gets richer over time.

Migration is built into the product. Train ingests what you have. Test validates it. Improve fills the gaps. You're productive on day one.

"How is this different from just using ChatGPT?"

ChatGPT generates from the internet. SparrowGenie generates from your company's curated Knowledge Hub with confidence scores. On top of that, we add Smart Mapping, structured collaboration, Instructions, Context Info, Competitor Info, Obligations, Project Health, and Workspace. ChatGPT is a text generator. SparrowGenie is a proposal operations platform.

ChatGPT is a writer. SparrowGenie is the entire proposal operation — with AI built on your knowledge, not the internet's.

"The Go/No-Go and Proposals features aren't built yet"

What's built today — Knowledge Hub (Train/Test/Improve), Projects (Smart Mapping, AI answers, collaboration, obligations), and Workspace — solves the core pain right now. Go/No-Go and Proposals are the compounding layer that makes the system self-improving. The value compounds over time, but the first RFP you run through SparrowGenie today is already faster.

The foundation and execution layer are live and delivering value now. The roadmap features make it compound — but you don't need them to start winning.

"How do you handle RFPs with 2000+ questions?"

SparrowGenie's Smart Mapping was built for exactly this scale. Upload the full RFP — the AI maps all questions regardless of volume. Combine that with Genie AI answering from the Knowledge Hub, and your team gets a head start on even the largest RFPs. Workspace lets SMEs manage their assigned portion without being overwhelmed by the full project.

We built for scale from day one. 50 questions or 2000 — same workflow, same speed.
