- Sales teams don’t struggle because they lack tools. 
  They struggle because selling is fragmented across too many of them.

- A single deal lives across calls, emails, notes, documents, reviews, approvals, and signatures. Each step is handled in a different place, by a different system, with context lost along the way. The result is slow cycles, repeated work, and deals that stall for reasons no one can clearly explain.

SparrowGenie exists to help sales teams close more deals, faster, using AI.

We start where the pain is most visible today: **RFPs and proposals**. These are high-stakes moments in a deal where speed, accuracy, and collaboration matter, yet teams still rely on manual work, copy-paste answers, and scattered reviews. SparrowGenie brings structure and intelligence to this process — helping teams draft, review, and submit proposals with less effort and more confidence.

But proposals are only the beginning.

Our long-term vision is to become the AI layer that supports the entire sales lifecycle. As SparrowGenie learns from proposals, conversations, and deal activity, it expands beyond documents into everything that slows a deal down — capturing context, preparing next steps, coordinating stakeholders, and removing friction wherever it appears.

Over time, SparrowGenie evolves from a proposal tool into a sales partner. It doesn’t replace salespeople. It clears the path for them. It handles the repetitive, the administrative, and the forgettable so salespeople can focus on building trust, understanding buyers, and closing.

As this vision becomes real, closing a deal feels less like managing chaos and more like following a guided flow. Work moves forward automatically. Teams stay aligned without chasing. Customers experience clarity instead of pressure.

We begin with RFPs and proposals.  
We expand into the full sales workflow.  
The outcome remains the same: **more deals closed, in less time, with AI doing the heavy lifting.**
